# Google outreach — ready-to-personalize drafts

Fill the `[[brackets]]`. Keep them short — referrals get read because they're
quick to act on, not because they're impressive. Your strongest asset is the
**ex-AWS → Google** connection: engineers refer people whose bar they already
trust, and "former AWS SDE" is exactly that signal.

> Tooling note: these are also generated automatically in the **Outreach** tab.
> Add the contact, put the shared context (e.g. "we overlapped at AWS
> OpenSearch") in the recruiter's **notes** field, and the referral draft will
> weave it in — see `src/pipeline/email_generator.py`.

## Personalization checklist (do NOT skip)

- [ ] Name the **specific req ID** — generic "any AI role" asks get ignored. Pull
      live reqs from your dashboard (the Google Careers source now feeds it).
- [ ] Name the **real shared context** — team, project, or manager. If you can't,
      it's a cold email, not a referral; use draft #2.
- [ ] One ask, one paragraph of fit, done. Never attach a résumé unless they ask
      — send the req ID so they refer you *into the system*, which is what counts.
- [ ] For India reqs, note you're an Indian citizen (no visa needed) — it removes
      the recruiter's biggest hesitation instantly.

## Where to find AWS → Google contacts

LinkedIn search → **Past company: Amazon** + **Current company: Google**, then
filter by 2nd-degree connections and your AWS org. Prioritize engineers/TLs over
recruiters for referrals (they carry more weight); use recruiters for the cold
path (#2) when you have no warm intro.
